3D printing:

I am very interested in 3D printing, especially the experimental side of it.
My printables profile: https://www.printables.com/@AyushSupe
My current setup:

  • Printer: Ender 3 V2
  • Slicer: Orca Slicer
  • CAD Software: Solidworks
Mods:
  • Raspberry Pi 3B+ running mainsail OS (idk I think its some Debian-based linux distro)
  • Cooling fan for the Raspberry Pi
  • BLTouch bed leveling probe
  • Bimetal heat-break (made of copper and titanium)
  • Microswiss MK8 heaterblock
  • 0.2mm and 0.4mm MK8 hardened steel nozzles
  • 5015 part cooling fans
  • 4028 sunon hotend fan (I don't like heat creep)
  • Custom fan driver circuit for the hotend fan (I accidentally killed one of the fan ports :( )
  • Hero me gen 7 cooling system
  • Capricon PTFE tubing
  • Direct drive
  • 2 KUSBA V2 accelerometers running rampon firmware (USB-C good I2C bad)
  • BTT SKR mini E3 V3 mainboard
  • TMC2209 stepper motor drivers
  • Buzzer (yey now the printer has sound)
Firmware and plugins:
  • Running the bleeding edge v2 fork of Kalico firmware (has a lot of experimental features like input smoothers and nonlinear pressure advance that klipper does not have)
  • Mainsail
  • klipper TMC-autotune
  • Klippain Shake and Tune
  • Klipper Adaptive Meshing Purging
Filaments I have used:
  • Overture Super PLA Plus (Amazing filament, pretty strong, a bit expensive though)
  • Overture PLA (Pretty good as well)
  • Transparent Overture PETG (Pretty good and strong, have to apply glue to the bed as a releasing agent and print slow)
  • Creality PLA (I don't really like it that much. It has a weird consistency)
Slicers that I have used:
  • Orca Slicer (Best slicer ever. So many settings. )
  • Prusa Slicer (Good but I could not figure out how to make the infill speed faster)
  • Bambu Studio (Pretty good but Bambu is not a good company)
  • FlashPrint 5 (works ig)
  • Ultimaker Cura (Good slicer for beginners. Way less settings)
  • Cura Lulzbot Edition (Ok slicer. Not that many options but works)
  • Makerbot Print (Makerbot printers bad. Very old and proprietary. Ancient stepper motor drivers)
CAD software that I have used:
  • Solidworks (Solidworks is amazing)
  • Blender (I know that it is not CAD software but this is amazing. I have been using this for at least 15 years since blender 3.7)
  • Onshape (very similar to Solidworks but worse)
  • Autodesk Inventor (pretty average)
  • FreeCAD (Also pretty average. Weird UI)
  • OpenSCAD (Very interesting software. I haven't used it that much. I probably should. Very good for math related things. )
  • Fusion360 (idk why but I hate Fusion)
Notable Mistakes:
  • I fried one of the fan ports by shorting the fan accidentally (transistor released the magic smoke)
  • My screwdriver fell while I was replacing the nozzle (like I was reassembling the cooling system) (This has to be done while the heaterblock is hot) and it shorted the heater which somehow killed the MCU on the stock motherboard (melted a hole in it)
  • I cut the heat-break in half (ye idk)
